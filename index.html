    📐 How to take the perfect photo

    1 · The angle

    The 30° tilt is for a single photo. If you'll add a side photo for depth, take this top photo straight down instead. It measures length and width more accurately, and the side photo handles height.

    2 · Where to put your hand

    Plate Hand beside the rim, fingertips level with the middle of the plate.
    Sandwich Lay it flat with its long side next to your hand. Add "2 slices cheese" etc. in extras.
    Bowl Hand right next to the bowl. Deep bowls hide depth, so tilt a little more to show the inside.
    Bread & drinks Whole loaf in view. For tea or juice, type the sugar or cup size in extras.

    3 · Avoid these

    Too far to the side Food looks shorter than it is. Come up higher.
    Hand lifted or nearer the phone It looks bigger, so the food looks smaller. Keep it flat on the table.

    4 · Side photo for depth (optional)

    • Stand your hand on its little-finger edge, palm facing the phone, fingers pointing at the food.
    • Lower the phone to table height so you see the food from the side.
    • With a side photo, take the top photo straight down.
    • Best for bowls, sandwiches, burgers and piled-up plates. Flat food only needs one photo at 30°.
